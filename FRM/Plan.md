# FRM 30-Day Reading Plan

Goal: Read all chapters of four textbooks within 30 days while keeping flexibility for allocation.

Books and chapters:
- Fundamentals of Risk Management — 11 chapters
- Quantitative Analysis — 15 chapters
- Financial Markets & Products — 20 chapters
- Valuation and Risk Models — 16 chapters

Total chapters: 62. Suggested pace: 2–3 chapters/day (average ≈2.1), adjust by difficulty and available time.

Weekly checkpoints:
- [ ] Week 1 (Days 1–7): ~14 chapters
- [ ] Week 2 (Days 8–14): ~14 chapters
- [ ] Week 3 (Days 15–21): ~16 chapters
- [ ] Week 4 (Days 22–27): ~12 chapters
- [ ] Reserve/Overflow Days (Days 28–30): review, consolidate weak topics, complete any remaining chapters, practice questions

Flexible approach (how to use this plan):
- Pick chapters from any book each day to match difficulty/energy; aim to average 2–3 chapters/day.
- If a chapter is long or dense, split it across days and mark sub-goals (e.g., read half today).
- Use weekly checkpoints to rebalance: if behind, increase to 2–3 chapters/day for 2–3 days; if ahead, use extra time for review.
