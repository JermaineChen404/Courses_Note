# Question 2

## (a)

*   Contribution Margin per unit = Selling Price per unit - Variable Expense per unit
*   Contribution Margin per unit = $20 - $8 = $12
*   CM Ratio = Contribution Margin per unit / Selling Price per unit
*   CM Ratio = $12 / $20 = **0.60 or 60%**

## (b)
*   Break-Even Sales ($) = Fixed Expenses / CM Ratio
*   Break-Even Sales ($) = $180,000 / 0.60 = **$300,000**

## (c)

For every additional unit sold, the net operating income will increase by the contribution margin per unit, assuming fixed costs remain constant.

*   Increase in Contribution Margin = 4,500 units × $12 CM per unit
*   Increase in Contribution Margin = $54,000

Since fixed expenses do not change, the change in net operating income is **$54,000**.

## (d)

### (i)
*   Degree of Operating Leverage = Contribution Margin / Net Operating Income
*   Degree of Operating Leverage = $300,000 / $120,000 = **2.5**

### (ii)
*   Percentage Increase in NOI = Degree of Operating Leverage × Percentage Increase in Sales
*   Percentage Increase in NOI = 2.5 × 20% = **50%**

A 20% increase in sales is expected to lead to a 50% increase in net operating income.
