## 📝 Course Overview

### Description
An introduction to financial risk management. Topics include measuring market risks, statistical properties of returns, volatility modeling, Value at Risk (VaR), Expected Shortfall, historical simulation, option pricing, and credit risk. Students will learn to use **R** for risk management tasks.

### Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)
By the end of this course, students should be able to:
- Understand theoretical concepts and choose appropriate risk management techniques.
- Apply statistical methods to solve real-world problems and interpret results.
- Make and communicate data-driven risk management decisions.

### Core Questions
- What is risk? How do we measure and predict it?
- Why not just avoid risk?
- Bank account vs. Stock market?
- How to (and not to) use options?
